# ER-006 Technical Analysis Semantics

TradeLens evaluates technical entry quality, not a company's fundamental or
long-term investment quality.

`Wait` means the current technical entry is unattractive or incomplete. Typical
reasons include thin resistance headroom, poor reward/risk, an unsteady
pullback, or insufficient confirmation while the broader trend is not broken.

`Avoid` remains appropriate for a genuinely broken technical setup, such as a
bearish trend that has lost its long-term average. It means avoid opening the
technical trade under the current evidence; it is not a fundamental judgment
that the company or its long-term thesis is bad.

Recommendation explanations must be traceable to the supplied EMA, RSI,
support, and resistance values. Disagreement with external research is not by
itself a defect: a positive longer-term thesis and an unattractive current
technical entry can both be valid statements.
